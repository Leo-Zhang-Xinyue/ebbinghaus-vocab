Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

EbbinghausVocab ("the app") is a vocabulary learning app. This policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and your rights.

1. Data You Provide

When you create an account, we store your email address and a user ID via Supabase (our database provider). If you use the app as a guest, no account data is stored on our servers.

Vocabulary entries, study projects, and language preferences you create are stored on our servers if you are signed in, so they can sync across your devices. In guest mode, this data is stored only on your device.

2. Microphone

The app uses your microphone for pronunciation practice. Speech is processed entirely on your device using Apple's built-in speech recognition. No audio is recorded, stored, or transmitted to our servers or any third party.

3. Camera and Photos

The app can use your camera or photo library to extract vocabulary from images (e.g. photographing a page of text). Image recognition is performed on your device using Google ML Kit. No images are uploaded or stored by us.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — stores your account, vocabulary, and project data if you are signed in. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Wiktionary / FreeDictionary — word text is sent to fetch definitions and pronunciation audio. No personal data is transmitted.
  • MyMemory / Google Translate TTS — word text is sent for translation and audio. No personal data is transmitted.
  • Anthropic / OpenAI (optional) — if you provide your own API key, word text may be sent to these services. We never store your API key on our servers; it is kept only on your device. Pro plan users have AI requests routed through our server-side proxy; word text is sent to Anthropic or OpenAI on your behalf but is not stored by us.
  • Stripe — if you subscribe to Pro, payment is processed by Stripe. We do not store your card details. Stripe Privacy Policy

5. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, or any data not described above.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app. Guest-mode data can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
